GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is ₹219 charged for each language? +

No. ₹219 is a one-time program enrollment fee, not a separate fee for every language in your profile.

Which languages can I add? +

You can add languages you genuinely speak and understand well enough for assigned tasks. Options include Hindi, English, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Chhattisgarhi, Malayalam, Kannada, Punjabi, Urdu and others.

How are voice tasks approved? +

Submitted recordings are reviewed for script accuracy, pronunciation, completeness and recording quality. Eligible approved tasks then follow the current wallet workflow.

Can I contribute from home? +

Eligible tasks can be completed remotely. You will need to follow the instructions and recording requirements shown for each assigned task.

Where can I learn more about the program? +

Visit the How It Works page or contact Swayam Digital for support.
